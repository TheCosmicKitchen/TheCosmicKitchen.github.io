    Fret Grind

    free

    Slow-cooked main · guitar and music

    Ten stages from first holding a guitar to folk songs, with 2,500 public-domain tunes to play along with. Your microphone, or a guitar plugged in by USB, tastes every note. Learn to read the staff and the rhythm, change chords and train your ear, with theory and piano on the side. Keeps well offline.
